How Giggo works

Fast to start, safe by design — for both sides.

🔎 For workers

Build a profile in under 2 minutes, apply with one tap, agree a price, do the job, and get paid securely — building a real work history from job one. No CV, no ghosting.

📣 For posters

Post a task in a minute, see nearby rated people, book the right one, and pay in-app only when it's done. No recruiters, no long job-board listings.

Pay-in-app protection

Covered if something goes wrong — cash jobs are not.

Why pay through Giggo? Contact details stay hidden until a booking is confirmed, and only in-app jobs count toward ratings, work history and protection. Moving to WhatsApp or cash means no proof, no protection, and no help if something goes wrong.
