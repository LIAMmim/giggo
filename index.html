§ I

Three steps, in brief.

From sign-up to paid in a single afternoon. The whole thing is designed to be quicker than making a cup of tea.

01 — Sign up

Build a profile in two minutes.

Name, age, where you live, what you can do. Pick a few skills. Under 18? A guardian signs off, and we only show you age-appropriate jobs.

02 — Apply

Tap the job. We handle the rest.

No cover letter. No interview unless the poster asks. Your profile and your rating are what they see — and both grow with every job.

03 — Get paid

Money sits in escrow until it's done.

The poster pays Giggo the moment they book you. When the work is marked complete, the funds land with you. No chasing. No cash.

§ II

Trust is the product.

Nothing that keeps you safe is optional. If a job is on Giggo, every one of these is on.

A — Identity

Verified people on both sides.

Workers and posters alike are checked before anything happens. Fake accounts don't last the week.

B — Escrow

Nobody holds the money but Giggo.

No cash swap. No bank-transfer hope. You either pay through Giggo and you're covered, or you take your chances off-app.

C — Both ways

Everyone has a reputation.

Workers rate posters. Posters rate workers. Two-sided from the very first gig.

Why pay through Giggo? — Jobs paid in cash or on WhatsApp don't count toward your rating, your work history, or Giggo protection. Keep it on-platform and we've got your back.
