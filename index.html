How it works

Three steps between you and your first gig.

Quicker than making a cup of tea. Designed for anyone from age 12, with no CV required.

01

Build a profile

Two minutes, no CV. Pick your skills, your area, and your hours. Under 18? A guardian signs off and we only show you jobs that fit.

02

Apply in one tap

Browse local jobs. Tap Apply. Your profile and rating are all they see — no cover letters, no interviews unless the poster asks.

03

Do it. Get paid.

Payment sits with Giggo until the job is signed off. Then it lands with you. Build a real work history from gig one.

Trust & safety

Built so you never worry about getting paid.

Every job on Giggo has these four things on. None of them are optional.

Verified people, both sides

Workers and posters are checked before anything happens. Fake accounts don't last.

Pay held in escrow

Posters pay Giggo the moment they book you. Funds release the second the job is signed off.

Two-way ratings

You rate the poster. They rate you. Everyone's reputation starts building from the very first gig.

Under-18 safeguards

Guardian approval, age-appropriate jobs only, and payouts linked to a guardian account. We never ask young people for bank logins.
